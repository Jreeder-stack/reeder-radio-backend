## Proposed finite limits for subsequent repair items

These are explicit starting design values, **not implemented settings, measured service guarantees or changes to emergency policy**. Keep them configurable, validate them under fake time, and tune against the supervised pilot evidence. Existing capture limits are not changed in 01-01.

| Operation | Proposed initial bound |
| --- | --- |
| Per-unit queue | Eight accepted waiting turns per unit; 64 total; eight concurrent workers. Reject overload explicitly rather than silently accumulating. |
| Queue waiting | Ten seconds maximum waiting age; preserve capture order and explicitly expire blocked work. |
| Speech recognition after PTT end | Twelve-second total application budget including normalization; reserve one second for SDK cleanup. Late callbacks have no authority. |
| Optional transcription normalization | Three seconds inside the recognition budget; on failure retain the raw transcript, not invented content. |
| Intent planning | 6.5 seconds inside the remaining turn budget; no unsafe deterministic fallback on timeout. |
| CAD request | Eight seconds through full body consumption. Read-back gets at most eight seconds within the same overall budget. Submitted-write timeout is unknown until reconciled. |
| Turn freshness | Thirty seconds from captured PTT end for accepting new side effects. Recheck at asynchronous boundaries and immediately before execution; a new arrival time does not refresh old speech. |
| Speech synthesis | Five seconds; cancellation/cleanup bounded to one second. |
| Outbound delivery | Three-second floor-acquisition attempts through existing controls; response relevance rechecked before transmission; queued output expires after 20 seconds. Do not expire a real emergency because dialogue expires. |
| Clarification | Thirty seconds from observed transmission completion, at most two clarification retries before a clear terminal/handoff state. No claim that transmission proves the officer heard it. |
| Response size | Routine responses target two sentences and no more than 15 seconds; preserve critical identifiers and use bounded continuation when needed. |

Stage limits do not add together to extend the absolute turn budget. A request without enough remaining budget must fail safely before starting a write. Canceling a conversation cannot undo an already-submitted write. Retry/idempotency gaps belong to 03-06, not a silent schema change here.
