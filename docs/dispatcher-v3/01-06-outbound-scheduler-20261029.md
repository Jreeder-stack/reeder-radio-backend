## Implementation
- `src/dispatcher-v3/outboundScheduler.js` implements one bounded **in-memory outbound lane per V3 runtime**, a finite priority FIFO with interruptible active routine output and explicit receipt states. Queue admission caps at **32 waiting messages**, waiting-item relevance expires at **20 seconds**, and an active output is interrupted after **22 seconds**. The queue never holds the 01-03 CAD processing lease. No distributed cross-process queue is claimed.
- `src/dispatcher-v3/liveRuntime.js` enqueues routine semantic replies and radio-hail prompts instead of awaiting radio injection in the CAD turn. Separate `_queueReply` records immutable turn ID, center-bound recipient, channel, runtime generation, and original trusted credential source. Newer trusted intake for the same unit invalidates an older routine reply if it has not been fully delivered. Queued source validation rechecks session/radio credential, enabled channel grant and current actor through 01-05's existing `revalidatePttActor` before transmission. Other units remain independent.
- Existing physical-emergency and floor-control policies remain authoritative. `_alert` routes through a priority outbound lane, interrupting routine AI output but **never forcing the radio floor away from another authorized speaker**. Alert priority is not invalidated merely because the same unit submitted a newer routine transmission. No audio transport, Opus framing, codec, bridge, Android firmware or CAD authorization changes were made.
- `_speak` and the emergency tone/audio sender remain the low-level injectors, with a relevance/abort check before floor acquisition, after synthesis and before each frame. They use the existing radio floor grant/rearm/release and 01-04 bounded synthesis stage; they do not bypass the floor or inject stale audio after cancellation. The outbound lane detaches finished 01-05 turn context and 01-04 turn budget, substituting its own generation, TTL and interruptible delivery validation. Stop/restart clears queued output, aborts active injection and rejects old-generation work.
- `getPipelineStatus().outbound` reports queue depth, active output, configured limits and counters. `outbound_queued`, `outbound_speaking`, `outbound_completed`, `outbound_interrupted`, `outbound_expired` and `outbound_failed` are state/outcome concepts; terminal outcomes use `outbound_*` diagnostic events. **Completed means the intended frames were injected through the relay**, not that a remote radio confirmed receiving/hearing them. An interrupted or expired prompt is never counted as delivered.
- The existing mocked audit harness explicitly waits for outbound delivery when testing radio output or cleaning up timers. This preserves the historical assertions without restoring blocking CAD behavior. Tests that inspect only CAD execution do not require speech transmission to finish.
